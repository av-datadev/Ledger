Deleting your Brick Book account

Brick Book · Last updated 5 August 2026

The quickest way is inside the app. Open Brick Book, go to the Data tab, find Shared ledger, and tap Delete my account. You will be asked to type DELETE to confirm. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in — your email address and your account itself
  • Your payments, bills and their line items, stock records, people and vendors, contract details, bank details, budget and house address
  • Every photograph you uploaded — bills, cheques, diary pages and QR codes
  • Any notification tokens for your devices
  • Any contractor profile and site links belonging to you, and the rows and bill photos shared on them

What happens to a ledger you share with other people

A household ledger usually belongs to more than one person. Deleting your account removes your access and your sign-in, and leaves the ledger itself with whoever is still in it — their records are not yours to erase.

When the last member of a household deletes their account, nobody can reach that ledger any more, so it is deleted in full: every entry, bill, stock record, person and photograph.

What is not affected

The copy held on your own phone is separate. Deleting your account from within the app clears that device as well. If you delete your account by writing to us instead, remove the app from your phone — or use Data → Clear all data — to clear the local copy.

Keep a copy first if you want one: Data → Backup & restore will download the whole ledger as a JSON file (including photographs) or as an Excel workbook.

How long it takes

Deleting from inside the app is immediate. There is no waiting period, no grace period, and no archived copy kept afterwards. Routine infrastructure backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten on their own schedule and are never used to restore a deleted account.
