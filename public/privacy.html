Privacy Policy

Brick Flow · Last updated 5 August 2026

In short. Brick Flow is a ledger for building a house. It runs on your phone and keeps your records on it. Nothing leaves the device unless you choose to sign in. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and your records are never sold or shared for marketing.

Who we are

Brick Flow is made and operated by Apoorv Verma. For anything in this policy, write to apoorvverma0396@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your device

This happens whether or not you sign in, and it is how the app works offline:

  • Payments you record — date, amount, category, description, payment mode, who paid, and your notes
  • People and vendors you add — name, role, phone number, ID number, contract terms, and, if you enter them, bank details (bank name, account holder, account number, IFSC, UPI ID)
  • Bills and their line items, stock records, your budget, and the house address
  • Photographs you attach — bills, cheques, diary pages, UPI QR codes
  • Preferences — light or dark theme, text size, and whether the AI reader is switched on

All of this sits in the app's own storage on that device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes it.

What is uploaded — and only if you sign in

Signing in is optional. The app is completely usable without an account; the sign-in exists so that several phones in a family can share one live ledger.

If you do sign in, the records listed above are copied to our cloud database so your other phones can see them, together with:

  • Your email address, used only to send your sign-in code and identify your account. There is no password.
  • A notification token for that device, only if you switch notifications on.

Hosting is provided by Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) in its Tokyo region, ap-northeast-1. Photographs are held in private storage: each time one is displayed the app requests a short-lived signed link, and the files are not publicly reachable.

Reading bills and handwriting with AI

This feature is off by default and is switched on per device.

When it is on, the photograph you are scanning — a printed bill, a PDF invoice, a handwritten slip, a cheque, or a timber size list — is sent over the internet to Google's Gemini API to be read, and the text that comes back fills in the form for you. The picture is sent for the purpose of reading it and for nothing else.

Switch it off, under Data → Reading handwritten notes, and no photograph leaves your device; the app falls back to reading printed bills on the phone itself. Because the setting is stored per device and is not synced, one person turning it on never turns it on for anyone else sharing the ledger.

Google's handling of anything sent to that API is governed by Google's own terms.

Sharing a site with a contractor

You can link one building site to a contractor. When you do, both of you see only the rows recorded against that shared site and any bill photograph attached to them. The rest of your ledger — every other vendor, your budget, and the People tab with its bank details — is never visible to them, and their other sites are never visible to you. Revoking the link ends that access immediately, photographs included.

Who else can see your data

  • Supabase — hosting, database, sign-in and file storage
  • Google — only the photographs you choose to scan, and only while the AI reader is switched on
  • Vercel — serves the app itself; it does not receive your ledger

No one else. There are no advertising networks, no analytics providers and no data brokers involved, and we do not sell your data or use it to build a profile of you.

What we never collect

Your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, the other apps on your phone, advertising identifiers, and any behavioural or usage analytics. The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a bill.

How long it is kept

On your device, until you delete it. In the cloud, until you delete your account.

Deleting your account and your data

You can do this yourself, inside the app: Data → Shared ledger → Delete my account. It removes your sign-in and your records from the cloud, and cannot be undone.

If other people still share your household ledger, deleting your account removes your access only and leaves their books intact. When the last member of a household deletes their account, that ledger and its photographs are removed completely.

If you can no longer open the app, you can request deletion here instead.

Children

Brick Flow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

You can see everything held about you from inside the app, export the whole ledger as a JSON or Excel file at any time from the Data tab, correct anything by editing it, and delete all of it as described above. To ask anything else, write to the address below.
