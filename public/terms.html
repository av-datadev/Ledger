What Brick Book is

Brick Book is an app for keeping track of the money, materials and people involved in building or renovating a house. It is provided free of charge by Apoorv Verma. There is nothing to buy inside it and no subscription.

Your account

The app works without an account. If you want to share one ledger across several phones, you sign in with your email address and a code we send you — there is no password. Keep access to that email address secure, since anyone who can read it can sign in as you.

You may delete your account at any time from the Data tab. See deleting your account.

Your records are yours

Everything you put into Brick Book belongs to you. We claim no ownership of it and do not use it for any purpose other than running the app for you. You can export the whole ledger at any time as a JSON file or an Excel workbook.

Keep your own backups

Brick Book is offered as-is, with no guarantee of availability. Records kept only on a device are lost if that device is lost, and contractor site books in particular are stored on the device alone. Please take backups from the Data tab and keep them somewhere safe.

The bill and handwriting reader

The optional AI reader is a convenience, not an authority. It misreads things — handwriting especially, and tick marks or crossings-out most of all. Always check the figures it fills in against the paper before saving. We are not responsible for a payment recorded from a misread bill.

Sharing a site with a contractor

When an owner and a contractor link a site, each side records its own rows and neither can edit the other's. Where the two disagree, the app shows the disagreement; it does not decide who is right, and it is not a record of any legal agreement between you.

The contractor directory

The directory lists tradespeople who asked to be listed. We do not employ, vet, guarantee or supervise them, and we take no part in whatever you agree with them. Check references and terms yourself. If you are listed and want to be removed, write to the address below.
