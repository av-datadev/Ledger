# BRICK BOOK — DESIGN CONSTITUTION v1.1

## North star
> Make complexity feel physically organized.

Brick Book is an offline-first construction-finance operating system for long-running house projects in India.

Character: Solid · Calm · Precise · Trustworthy · Practical · Construction-specific · Intelligent · Premium.

Must not feel like: generic SaaS, generic fintech, ERP template, Material clone, iOS Settings, glassmorphism, AI dashboard, Dribbble concept.

## Ten laws
1. Hierarchy before decoration.
2. Every surface must earn its existence.
3. One dominant action per region.
4. Money is typography.
5. Metadata stays quiet.
6. Progressive disclosure is the default.
7. Existing system beats invention.
8. Mobile first, desktop enhanced.
9. Offline/sync state is human and visible.
10. Construction specificity must survive removal of the logo.

## Information choreography
What matters → What changed → What needs attention → What can I do → Detail → Advanced controls.

## Product relationships
Payment ↔ Person/Vendor
Payment ↔ Bill
Bill ↔ BOQ item
BOQ ↔ Stock
Person/Vendor ↔ Contract

## Formulas
Remaining stock = Received − Given out.
Contractor in-hand = Received − Spent.
Contract remaining = Contract value − Paid.

## Design priority
Comprehension → Speed → Trust → Density → Beauty.
Beauty must never damage the first four.
