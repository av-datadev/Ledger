Three years on one page

The house goes up.
So does the number.

Eight stages of a ground-plus-one, and what the book said at the end of each.

Spent by this stage
₹48,62,400
81% of the ₹60,00,000 budget set at the start
  1. Stage 01

    Land, drawings, approvals.

    Months of spending before a single brick is laid — the architect, the chalan, the levelling. Nothing to photograph, and the easiest money in the whole project to forget you spent.

    ₹6,15,000by the end of it · ₹6,15,000 this stage
  2. Stage 02

    Foundation and plinth.

    Steel and cement bought by the trip and paid for on the spot, mostly in cash. This is the stage where “where has it all gone?” gets asked for the first time.

    ₹10,75,000by the end of it · ₹4,60,000 this stage
  3. Stage 03

    The ground floor frame.

    Columns up, slab poured. The contractor’s running account overtakes every other line in the book this month, and never gives the position back.

    ₹16,15,000by the end of it · ₹5,40,000 this stage
  4. Stage 04

    Another floor, and the stair head.

    The same frame again, one level up. Identical work, identical cost — which you only know for certain because the first one was written down properly.

    ₹21,55,000by the end of it · ₹5,40,000 this stage
  5. Stage 05

    Brickwork, both floors.

    Six bays of masonry and the openings left in them. Forty-six payments to the contractor so far, every one of them against a name and a date.

    ₹24,55,000by the end of it · ₹3,00,000 this stage
  6. Stage 06

    Plumbing and wiring.

    Thirty-one plumbing payments and twenty-seven electrical, to four different people, across two floors. Two years from now, when a tap leaks, you will want that list.

    ₹32,35,500by the end of it · ₹7,80,500 this stage
  7. Stage 07

    Flooring, wood and marble.

    The stage that quietly doubles. Each slab photographed with its rate, so what arrives on site can be checked against what was agreed at the shop.

    ₹46,80,500by the end of it · ₹14,45,000 this stage
  8. Stage 08

    Paint, and the keys.

    ₹11,37,600 under the budget set three years earlier — and you can say so with certainty, because all 182 payments are in one book.

    ₹48,62,400by the end of it · ₹1,81,900 this stage
